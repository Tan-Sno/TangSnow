## 用户协议

1. 服务性质
本应用是一款本地网页辅助工具，提供网页渲染、扩展（来自 Mozilla 官方签名源 AMO）、二维码识别（含相机扫码与从相册选图）、将本地图片设为主页背景、生成页面 PDF、桌面版切换、标签页会话恢复（进程被系统回收后恢复上次打开的标签页，仅本地保存）、资料库（历史/书签/下载记录的管理与一键清空；清空下载记录不删除文件）、连接安全状态提示（完全在本机判断）与可选的「禁止截屏」等能力；不提供、不聚合、不审核任何网页内容；网页产生的缓存等浏览数据仅保存在设备本地（见隐私政策第 1、7 条）。

2. 同意机制与变更
首次安装或本协议/隐私政策更新时，应用会在使用前展示协议摘要，您需点击「同意并继续」方可使用；点击「不同意」将立即退出。协议如有变化将以应用内最新版本为准，并在下次启动时重新征得同意。

3. 扩展功能说明
扩展均来自 Mozilla 官方渠道并由 Mozilla 签名，本应用不提供、不引导安装任何第三方镜像或未签名扩展；部分扩展可能因官方地区合规限制而无法安装。
您也可以从设备本地导入 .xpi 文件安装：文件仍须携带 Mozilla 官方签名，未签名或签名不符的文件会被内核拒绝；本地导入仅在本机校验与安装，不绕过任何地区限制，也不视为本应用对第三方分发渠道的认可。

4. 搜索引擎与内容合规
默认搜索为国内可访问的持证搜索服务。境外搜索引擎、自定义搜索地址仅由您主动选择使用，请确保在自身所在地区合规的网络与法律环境下使用，并自行承担由此产生的责任。应用不提供任何规避网络审查的手段。

## English

1. Service nature

This app is a local browsing tool providing page rendering, Mozilla-signed AMO extensions, QR scanning (camera and from-gallery), using a local picture as the home background, saving pages as PDF, desktop-site switching and tab session restoration (restoring your last open tabs after the process is reclaimed, saved locally only) and a Library for managing and one-tap clearing of history, bookmarks and download records (clearing download records does not delete files). It also shows an on-device connection-security indicator and offers an optional “Block screenshots” switch. It does not host, aggregate or review any web content; browsing data such as the page cache is stored on your device only (see §§1 and 7 of the privacy policy).



2. Consent and changes

On first use, or when this agreement or the privacy policy changes, this summary appears before any use; you must tap “Agree and continue” to proceed, and “Disagree” exits immediately. Future changes restart the consent flow on next launch.



3. Extensions

All extensions come from the official Mozilla source and are Mozilla-signed; no third-party mirrors or unsigned extensions are offered. Some may be region-limited by Mozilla’s own policy.

You may also install a local .xpi file from your device: it must still carry a valid Mozilla signature — unsigned or mismatched files are rejected by the engine. Local import is verified and installed on-device only; it does not bypass any regional limits and is not an endorsement of third-party distribution channels.

Some ad- and content-filtering extensions (e.g. ad blockers) may differ in availability and legal status across jurisdictions; their publishers may restrict provision in certain regions to comply with local laws or binding court rulings. Whether to install or use such tools is your own judgment and responsibility. The app neither bundles nor recommends any ad-blocking extension: the curated catalog lists privacy and utility extensions only.



4. Search engines and compliance

The default search engine is an accessible, licensed domestic service. Overseas search engines and custom search URLs are used only when you actively choose them; you are responsible for using them in a compliant network and legal environment. The app offers no tool to circumvent network regulation.



5. Default browser

You may explicitly request to set TangSnow as the default browser in Settings → Advanced. The app never changes the system default on its own.



6. User responsibilities

You agree to comply with the laws of your jurisdiction and to refrain from accessing, distributing or storing illegal content (including content endangering state security, obscenity, or infringing others’ IP and personal information). Downloaded files should be verified by yourself.



7. Protection of minors

Minors should use this app under adult guidance.



8. Infringement and reporting

Pages and extensions are provided by their respective owners; contact them first for takedown requests. For issues about this app itself, contact us via the project's GitHub repository.



9. Disclaimer

Disputes or losses caused by third-party sites, extensions, files you download, or overseas/custom search engines you enable are not the responsibility of this app’s developer.



10. Open source

This app uses Mozilla GeckoView (MPL 2.0), OkHttp and ZXing (Apache License 2.0); see “Open source notice” for statements and license links.
